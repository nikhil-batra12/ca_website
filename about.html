Our Story

Building Financial Futures with Trust

H. Khanna & Co. was established in 1991 with a vision to provide exceptional accounting, tax consultancy, and financial advisory services to businesses and individuals.

With over three decades of experience, we have grown into a trusted firm serving clients across Delhi NCR and beyond. Our UDYAM-registered practice combines traditional values with modern accounting practices, ensuring compliance and financial success for all our clients.

We believe that every individual and business deserves access to quality financial advice. This belief drives us to stay updated with the latest regulations, invest in technology, and maintain the highest standards of professional ethics.
